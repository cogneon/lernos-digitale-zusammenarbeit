## Kata 4 - Communication: Choose the right communication channel

In the previous exercises, you reflected on your communication. In this
Kata, you will work on how you can improve your communication. You will
learn how to select suitable communication channels to enable fast and
effective collaboration with internal and external parties.

The focus is on choosing the communication tool that best conveys your
message. Some tools are better because they are fast. Other tools are
better because they carry emotional undertones. Part of good
communication is choosing a good communication medium.

#### Preparation (individual work)

- Read the Kata "Choose the right communication channel" (see below),
  do the exercises.

#### Kata "Choose the right communication channel!"

In the following, you will find out how you can best reach your goals with the help of communication. 

- Take another look at the two inputs from Kata 3:
      - [Info block Synchronous vs. asynchronous
      communication](5-02-Communication.md#basics-synchronous-asynchronous-communication)
  
      - [Info block Push vs. pull communication](5-02-Communication.md#basics-push-vs-pull-communication)
  

- As a basis for the task, take a look at the various subject areas that
  will help you to select a suitable communication channel.

    - [Subject area: E-mail](5-02-Communication.md#subject-area-e-mail)  
    - [Subject area: Chat](5-02-Communication.md#subject-area-chat)  
    - [Subject area: Meetings](5-02-Communication.md#subject-area-meetings)  
    - [Subject area: Tasks](5-02-Communication.md#subject-area-tasks)

- In the next step, answer the following questions using an example
  topic:

  1.  What do I want to achieve? (**goal**)

      - Create understanding for a topic?

      - Assign a task?

      - Pass on information? / Status update

      - Discuss a topic?

      - Reach a decision?

      - Should the information be available and retrievable in the long
      term?  
      Are there perhaps requirements for archiving / revision security
      for documentation purposes (e.g. for orders)?

      - Convey a lot of information (that could be lost in a phone call,
      for example)?

      - Detailed information that requires preparation?

  2.  How quickly and urgently do I need an answer? (**urgency**)

  3.  Who is my communication aimed at? (**target group**)

      - Individual / Group?

      - Do I know the right contact person?

      - Who is the target group?

  4.  Is it an emotional or unemotional topic? (**Emotions**)

      - Caution: Emotional topics are difficult to place and control
      without direct eye contact!

      - Public visibility is not always useful: criticism, for example, is
      sometimes better placed in person.

  5.  How **much time and effort** do I need to prepare / does my
    counterpart need to answer?

      - How complex is the topic? (Complexity)

      - Is there a great need for explanation?

        Tip: Save information in a document or presentation (meeting).

  6.  "Does it have to be a meeting if everyone listens to one person
    speak?" 😉  
    (For more information, see [Theory: Organizing meetings efficiently](5-04-Meetings.md)).
