## Kata 1 - How do we work together in the Circle?

#### Your entry into digital collaboration

In the preparatory meeting, you have already started discussing your
collaboration with your learning group. In this Kata, we present a
structure for all digital collaborations: the Collaboration Canvas. The
Collaboration Canvas is a framework for building group agreements and
collaboration. Think of your learning circle as a collaboration group -
how will you work together? ...communicate with each other? ...make
decisions? ...share your knowledge? At the end of this learning
journey, you will be able to use these tools with all your other working
groups.

#### Preparation (individual work)

- Read the Kata "How do we work together?", do the exercises.  
Tip: You will always find the task text for the Kata after the agenda

#### Agenda (circle/group work)

- **Check-in:** *(10 minutes)*  
  What has been on your mind this past week in relation to digital
  collaboration?  
  Two-minute timebox per Circle member.

- **Main topic**: *(45 minutes)*

    - Present your results and findings from Kata 1 - How do we work
  together? (below) to each other and discuss them

    - Develop your rules for collaboration in the [collaboration canvas](5-01-Cooperation.md#collaboration-canvas)

    - Write your conclusions from week 0 in the canvas.

    - During the following weeks of the circle, consider whether rules in
  the canvas should be adapted or expanded. After each week / journey
  stage, take the time to fill it out step by step.

- **Check-out:** *(5 minutes)*  
  What will you do before the next meeting?

#### Kata "How do we work together?"

- To prepare, read through the content in the chapter [Basics: Collaboration](5-01-Cooperation.md).

- Take a look at the **Collaboration Canvas** (see below). The canvas
  offers you the opportunity to record the new impulses and
  recommendations you have learned for implementation in your daily
  working life while working with the guidelines. Some topics (e.g.
  sharing documents) will be addressed in more detail during the course
  of the Circle.
