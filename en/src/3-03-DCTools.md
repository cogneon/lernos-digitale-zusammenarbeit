## What are digital collaboration tools?

The digital collaboration of teams is made possible by digital
collaboration tools. This allows teams of people from inside and outside
the organization to come together to work on projects and topics.

Digital collaboration tools are applications that enable digital
collaboration within the team, department and/or company. They also
promote internal communication. Well-known examples include: MS Teams,
Slack, Planner, Asana and Mural.

Notes on dealing with shadow IT:

- In this guide, we provide examples of tools. You will need to check
  with your organization and, if necessary, use them in a
  company-compliant manner.

- The same applies to companies that adapt this guide to your system.
  They are responsible here for clearly saying which tools are allowed
  and which are not.
