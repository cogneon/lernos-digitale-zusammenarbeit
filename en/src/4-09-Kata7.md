## Kata 7 - Making decisions

In every collaboration, decisions must be made together. The choice of
approach or method has a major influence on whether the decision is made
quickly and purposefully or whether the team gets bogged down in endless
discussions without reaching a common consensus.

#### Preparation (individual work)

- Read the Kata "Making a decision" (below) and do the exercises.

#### Agenda (circle/group work)

- **Check-in:** *(10 minutes)*  
  What has been on your mind this past week in relation to digital
  collaboration?  
  Two-minute timebox per Circle member.

- **Main topic**: *(45 minutes)*

  Present your results and findings from "Kata 7 - Making a decision"
  (below) to each other and discuss them.

- **Check-out:** *(5 minutes)*  
  What will you do before the next meeting?

#### Kata "Making decisions"

- Read the theory

  1.  How does decision-making take place digitally in your team, your
    workplace (if you don't meet in person)?

  2.  What is going well?

  3.  What is not going so well?

  4.  What methods/tools do you use for this?

- Think about how you ask the question. ([Theory: Types of
  questions](5-05-Decisions.md#question-types))

- Find a suitable tool for your question and try it out in the Circle

  Choose a [tools](5-05-Decisions.md#tools)/[method](5-05-Decisions.md#methods) to make a decision
  on your question. Take another look at [Kata 4 - Choose a suitable channel](4-06-Kata4.md).

#### If you want to do more

- Familiarize yourself with other tools and methods and think about
  where you could use them.

- Research further methods for decision-making and try them out in the
  next meetings of your Circle or in your team.

- Reflect on the result in the relevant group to see whether this method
  has helped you come to a decision.
